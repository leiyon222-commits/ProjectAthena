# Remaining Hypothesis Map

Candidate counts were measured on 23,496 hourly observation points from 2022-09-01 through 2026-08-04 without reading any outcome or expected-R column. Counts assess feasibility only and were not used to tune thresholds.

| Priority | Hypothesis | Untested features | Structural difference and mechanism | Not a threshold retry | Candidates | Feasibility | Decision |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Cross-asset dispersion expansion | `strength_usd_dispersion_1h`, `strength_jpy_dispersion_1h`, H1 pressure | First crossing above the trailing 120-H1 75th percentile represents a sudden decomposition of normally coherent FX moves; USDJPY follows the contemporaneous aggregate pressure direction. | Earlier impulse used pressure zero-cross and agreement only; it did not condition on rolling cross-pair dispersion state. | 3,594 | High | Adopt as `ATH-XDISP-EXPAND-001`. |
| 2 | High-dispersion convergence mean reversion | Same dispersion fields plus preceding H1 pressure | A one-hour collapse from above the prior 75th percentile to below the prior median indicates resolution of cross-pair disagreement; USDJPY reverses the pressure direction observed before convergence. | This is a dispersion-state collapse and contrarian mechanism, not a pressure threshold change. | 2,004 | High | Adopt as `ATH-XDISP-CONVERGE-001`. |
| 3 | H1/H4/D1 pressure agreement transition | `strength_usdjpy_pressure_1h/4h/1d`, agreement fields | First transition into identical nonzero pressure signs across three horizons represents multi-scale information confirmation and is followed in the agreed direction. | Earlier experiment observed only H1 zero crossing and never required horizon agreement. | 4,989 | High | Adopt as `ATH-XHORIZON-ALIGN-001`. |
| 4 | H1/D1 disagreement resolution | H1 and D1 pressure | When H1 previously opposed D1 and then realigns with an unchanged D1 sign, the short-horizon dislocation has resolved; trade in the D1 direction. | Uses a two-state mismatch-to-resolution transition rather than any absolute pressure threshold. | 5,964 | High | Adopt as `ATH-XHORIZON-RESOLVE-001`. |
| 5 | Agreement-rate shock | `strength_all_agreement_1h`, H1 pressure | Transition from neutral 40–60% breadth to strong >=75% or <=25% breadth represents a sudden broad directional consensus; trade with the new consensus. | Earlier impulse required an H1 pressure zero cross; this event is driven by breadth-state change and does not require a zero cross. | 3,743 | High | Adopt as `ATH-XAGREE-SHOCK-001`. |

| 8 | Agreement to disagreement | H1 agreement rate | Strong breadth becoming neutral may precede either consolidation or reversal. Existing four-hour directional labels do not encode a neutral/no-trade payoff, so a directional rule would be arbitrary. | Not excluded as “same”; excluded because feature transition lacks a uniquely signed mechanism. | 3,754 | Candidate-rich but directionally underidentified | Exclude. |
| 9 | Low-dispersion directional emergence | dispersion quartile and agreement rate | Entry into low dispersion while breadth is strong could indicate coherent direction, but its signal set overlaps the agreement-rate shock and differs mainly by an additional dispersion filter. | Adding the low-dispersion threshold would be a nested filter on priority 5 rather than a separate mechanism within the remaining slot budget. | 1,603 | Adequate | Exclude to avoid nested multiple comparison. |

The mandatory information structures are therefore all evaluated: dispersion expansion/convergence, pressure agreement/disagreement, both agreement-state transition directions, H1/H4/D1 hierarchy, W1/M1 conflict, and dispersion combined with USDJPY direction. Six are selected because they have a signed market mechanism and are not nested threshold variants.
